Application

Apply for a VillageServer card

Missionaries and field partners can apply to receive a microSD card carrying the full offline library — Scripture, audio Bible, gospel films, and teaching — in the language your community actually reads and hears. It works on an ordinary phone with no internet at all. The application takes about 15 minutes.

What we send you

  • A microSD card carrying the full offline library, loaded in your community's language
  • An SD card adapter, so the card fits a phone, a reader, or a computer

What you obtain locally

  • Phones, tablets, or computers to read the card
  • Televisions, projectors, and speakers for showings
  • Raspberry Pi servers, Wi-Fi hubs, and solar power
  • Satellite dishes, receivers, and installation

We are not able to send equipment or funding at this stage, and we would rather say so plainly than leave you waiting on something that is not coming. The card is genuinely the beginning — everything on the right can be built around it later, and your file stays with us as more becomes available.
